Sellsnap

Photograph a second-hand item and get an identification plus a fair price range, backed by recent comparable sales — so you're not guessing what to list it for.

Get notified

How it works

  • Camera capture Take a photo of the item you want to sell, right in the app.
  • Title, brand, category, condition Sellsnap identifies the item and estimates its condition from the photo.
  • A price range you can trust See a fair price range with the comparable sold listings and reasoning behind it — not just a single number.
  • Capture history Look back at everything you've captured and priced.

How privacy works

Sellsnap is an early preview. Right now, photos are sent to a cloud vision service to identify the item and generate a price range. We don't store your photos on our servers.

We're exploring an on-device version that wouldn't need to send photos anywhere. Full details are in our privacy policy.

Status

Sellsnap is an early preview / proof of concept. It's not a finished product yet — we're validating whether the identification and pricing are useful before investing further.
