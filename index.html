Our principles

On-device by default

Where it's technically possible, processing happens on your phone. Nothing needs to leave your device for the app to work.

No accounts unless needed

If a feature can work without a login, it does. Accounts only show up for things that genuinely require them, like optional social features.

Plain-language privacy

Our privacy policy says what actually happens to your data, in plain English — no legal fog. See it on our privacy page.

Who's behind Sotasci

Sotasci is run by Tejas Vyas, a software architect and computer vision researcher based in Toronto, with a background in enterprise systems and research on compact multimodal models. Sotasci is where those two interests meet: small, useful apps that run AI on your device instead of in someone else's cloud.
