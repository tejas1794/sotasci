Ember Quest

A habit tracker that plays like an idle RPG. The tasks you actually complete grow a character who battles monsters, gears up, and explores a world map — so your progress feels like progress.

  • Android
  • iOS
Join the beta

How it plays

  • Stats that grow from real tasks Your character auto-battles using four stats — strength, constitution, intelligence, and perception — that level up as you complete real-life tasks.
  • Four classes Pick a class that matches how you want to play, each leaning on a different mix of stats.
  • Zone-based world map Progress unlocks new zones, each gated by a boss fight you take on when you're ready.
  • Loot and equipment Boss fights and zones drop gear that changes how your character looks and performs.
  • Analytics that mean something Goal tracking, XP trends, activity heatmaps, and a story journal that logs your run.
  • An AI companion Suggests quests and gives tips based on your habits and progress.
  • Optional social features Add friends, form a party, or climb a leaderboard — entirely optional.
  • Streaks Keep momentum visible without shaming you for a missed day.

How privacy works

No account, no data leaves your phone — by default. The AI companion can run fully on-device using a small, bundled model, so quest suggestions and tips are generated locally.

If you'd rather use a larger cloud model, you can optionally supply your own API key from a provider of your choice. In that case, the prompts needed to generate suggestions are sent to that provider — full details are in our privacy policy.

Optional social features (friends, parties, leaderboards) only sync the information needed to make those features work, and only if you turn them on.

Status

Ember Quest is currently in testing on Android and iOS. We're looking for a small group of real testers before opening things up further.
