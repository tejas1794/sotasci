Support

Answers to common questions, plus how to reach us if you're stuck.

Habitography

Do I need an account to use Habitography?

No. The core game — your character, stats, quests, and journal — works fully offline with no account. An account is only relevant if you turn on optional social features like friends or leaderboards.

Does the AI companion send my data anywhere?

Not by default. The AI companion runs on your device using a bundled small model. It only sends data off your device if you choose to enable cloud AI with your own API key. See our privacy policy for details.

How do I join the beta?

Use our contact form and let us know you'd like in — we're onboarding testers in small batches.

I found a bug. What should I include in my report?

Please include: your device model and OS version (e.g. "Pixel 8, Android 15" or "iPhone 14, iOS 18"), the app version if you can find it in Settings, what you expected to happen, what actually happened, and steps to reproduce it. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot. Send it through our contact form.

Sellsnap

Is Sellsnap finished?

No — it's an early preview / proof of concept. Identification and pricing are actively being improved, and we're validating whether they're accurate and useful enough for everyday use.

Where does the price range come from?

From recent comparable sold listings for similar items, along with the rationale for why those listings were chosen as comparables.

Does Sellsnap store my photos?

No. Photos are sent to a cloud vision service to identify the item and are not stored by us afterward. See our privacy policy for details.

How do I get notified about updates?

Use our contact form to ask to be notified.
